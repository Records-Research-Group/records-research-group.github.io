Records Research Group

Records Research Group is an independent, non-commercial public-interest research effort examining the completeness and accessibility of Oregon government administrative and financial records — comparing what agencies publish or produce on request against federal audit filings (Single Audit / FAC data) and other public sources.

All findings, derived datasets, and research notes are published here at no cost. Work products are dedicated to the public domain (CC0) unless noted otherwise.

Current work

  • Lane County records accessibility study (Oct 2026–) — a systematic set of public-records requests to Lane County and Oregon state agencies covering vendor disbursements, payroll, election-administration statistics, and voter-list maintenance records. Status and notes: research note.
