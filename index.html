Records Research Group

Records Research Group is an independent, non-commercial public-interest research effort measuring the gap between what Oregon public-records law obligates and what desks actually do — fees, channels, waivers, productions, nonresponses, and petitions, all documented in a published request ledger.

All findings, derived datasets, and research notes are published here at no cost. Work products are dedicated to the public domain (CC0) unless noted otherwise.

Start here

  • Findings — eight documented findings from a controlled request sweep (163 requests, 130 bodies), each stated as claim / obligation / observed behavior / witnesses / what-it-does-not-establish.
  • Accessibility scoreboard — the per-request ledger the findings cite, as a dataset (CSV).
  • Request pages — permanent timelines for portal-ticketed threads.
