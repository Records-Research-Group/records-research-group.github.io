About

Who we are

Records Research Group is the public-facing name of an independent, non-commercial public-interest research effort based in Lane County, Oregon. The work examines the completeness and accessibility of Oregon government administrative and financial records — comparing what agencies publish or produce on request against federal audit filings (Single Audit / FAC data) and other public sources.

The research is not performed for any paying client. There is no subscription, license, or access fee of any kind — all publications are free at the point of access and dedicated to the public domain (CC0) unless noted otherwise. Any records fees are paid personally.

Methodology

Requests are drafted to seek records, not answers — "records sufficient to show" rather than narrative questions — and to accept existing exports where an agency already maintains them (check registers, payroll reports). Derived datasets are built only from public sources and are published with their provenance. The request/response process itself — dates, fees quoted and paid, waiver outcomes — is maintained as a dataset on the accessibility scoreboard.

Corrections policy

If we publish something wrong, we fix it. Factual errors reported to recordsresearchgroup@gmail.com are verified against source records; corrections are made to the affected page or dataset and noted with the date of the change. If a published dataset is revised, prior versions remain citable via their deposit DOI (see datasets).
