For journalists and researchers

Standing offer: findings and derived datasets from Records Research Group's Oregon records-accessibility work are available to local journalists and researchers covering the jurisdiction — Lane County and Eugene-area civic reporting in particular — at no cost and with no conditions on use.

What's available now

  • Lane County records accessibility study — a systematic set of ORS 192 requests measuring what routine administrative and financial information costs to obtain. Research note, per-request scoreboard, per-request pages.
  • Fees and waivers as data — four fee estimates totalling $1,284.59 across the October 2026 wave; fee-waiver outcomes tracked including Lane County's two 2026-11-02 denials grounded on the requester's "ability to distribute the information."
  • Derived datasets — Single Audit (FAC)-derived overlays for Oregon entities and Oregon DHS/OHA vendors (data/, CC0). Datasets carry stable URLs for citation; DOI'd versions are staged for deposit.

On request

Underlying correspondence metadata (dates, fee calculations, portal-ticket references) can be shared for verification. Requests for comment on the study's findings: recordsresearchgroup@gmail.com.
