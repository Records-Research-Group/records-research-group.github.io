# Records Research Group

Records Research Group is an independent, non-commercial
public-interest research effort examining the completeness and
accessibility of Oregon government administrative and financial
records — comparing what agencies publish or produce on request
against federal audit filings (Single Audit / FAC data) and other
public sources.

Findings, derived datasets, and research notes are published here and
at <https://records-research-group.github.io> at no cost.
