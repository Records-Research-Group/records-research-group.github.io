Lane County records accessibility study — October 2026

Research note. Posted 2026-10-30. Status: requests in flight.

What this is

A systematic set of Oregon Public Records Law (ORS 192.311 et seq.) requests to Lane County and Oregon state agencies, aimed at measuring how much routine administrative and financial information is visible to the public — and what it costs to get what isn't.

Early observations

  • Lane County routes requests through the NextRequest portal; substantive replies arrive as portal messages rather than directly from the custodian office.
  • The county applies an 8-question fee-waiver questionnaire (drawn from the Oregon AG's public-records manual) and asked specifically for the web address where findings are published — which is what prompted this site's creation.
  • The county was explicit that ORS 192 compels production of records, not answers to questions.
  • Estimated fees so far: $92.22 for the voter-records request. Whether the waiver arguments succeed is itself a datapoint.

Methods note

Derived datasets and reconciled views will be posted here as records arrive, alongside request/response correspondence metadata (dates, fees quoted and paid, response times) so the process is as measurable as the records themselves.
