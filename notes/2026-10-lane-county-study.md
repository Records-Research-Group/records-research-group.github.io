# Lane County records accessibility study — October 2026

*Research note. Posted 2026-10-30; updated 2026-11-02. Status: requests in flight.*

## What this is

A systematic set of Oregon Public Records Law (ORS 192.311 et seq.)
requests to Lane County and Oregon state agencies, aimed at measuring
how much routine administrative and financial information is visible
to the public — and what it costs to get what isn't.

## Observations

- Lane County routes requests through the NextRequest portal;
  substantive replies arrive as portal messages rather than directly
  from the custodian office.
- The county applies an 8-question fee-waiver questionnaire (drawn
  from the Oregon AG's public-records manual) before deciding waiver
  requests, and asked specifically for the web address where findings
  are published — which is what prompted this site's creation.
- The county was explicit that ORS 192 compels production of records,
  not answers to questions: a request framed as questions will be
  answered only to the extent existing records cover them.
- **Fee record as of 2026-11-02:** four estimates totalling
  **$1,284.59** — $92.22 (Clerk #26-975), $186.48 (County #26-971),
  $728.89 (Clerk #26-974), $277.20 (Eugene #PRR-2026-896). Fees paid
  to date: $0.
- **Waiver record as of 2026-11-02:** four waivers/reductions
  requested under ORS 192.324(5); **two denied** (both Lane County,
  same day, both citing the requester's limited distribution capacity
  under the *In Defense of Animals v. OHSU* discretionary standard);
  two pending (Clerk #26-975; Eugene #PRR-2026-896).
- Both Lane County denials invoked the requester's ability to
  disseminate findings — making the publication record itself part of
  this study's data. A dissemination plan documenting publication
  venues, format, cadence, and distribution channels has been
  prepared for submission as a supplemented public-interest showing.
- The county's no-charge payroll alternative (names, titles,
  departments — without wages) is itself an informative boundary:
  the same fields exist either way; the fee attaches to the wage
  columns specifically.
