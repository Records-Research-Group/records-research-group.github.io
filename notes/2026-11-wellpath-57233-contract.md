# Lane County jail medical contract #57233 (Wellpath LLC): $34.1M cumulative authority through FY2027

*Research note. Posted 2026-11-16. Dataset: [`../data/wellpath_57233_contract_ladder.csv`](../data/wellpath_57233_contract_ladder.csv). Request page: [`../requests/lane-county-26-1042.html`](../requests/lane-county-26-1042.html).*

## What this is

A document ladder of every instrument executed under Lane County
contract #57233 — medical, dental, and mental-health services at the
Lane County Adult Corrections facility, held by Wellpath LLC — from
its FY2023 base through seven amendments extending the term to
June 30, 2027. Built to make one number checkable: the total payment
authority the county has granted under this contract, and how that
authority grew.

## Source & method

- **Source:** the complete executed instrument file — base contract
  plus Amendments 1–7 — released by Lane County in two no-charge
  productions on public-records request #26-1042 (November 13 and
  November 16, 2026), under the Oregon Public Records Law
  (ORS 192.311 et seq.). The county's handling of this request was
  exemplary: each release arrived next-day, free, and the second
  released more instruments than were asked for.
- **Figures are contract authority** — the ceiling the county has
  obligated — not payments made. Lane County voucher-register data
  covering CY2023 through mid-2025 shows payments running at the
  contract's installment cadence.
- **Amendment 6 is an image-only scan.** Its contents — a staffing
  title adjustment carrying a proposed staffing matrix — were
  recovered by OCR. Every dollar figure in the ladder comes from a
  text-layered instrument; the OCR document affects no number.
- The sole-source history (2021 solicitation, one response,
  good-faith negotiation) comes from the county's own Board Order
  22-03-08 procurement memo.

## Findings

- **Cumulative authority through June 30, 2027: $34,123,588.24**
  across eight executed instruments.
- **Annual run-rate up 42%:** $5.49M (FY23) → $7.78M (FY27).
- **Some of the growth is new scope, not escalation.** Amendment 3
  (FY25) added a medication-assisted treatment program (~$296K/yr)
  and juvenile-facility medical services (~$862K/yr) — real new
  services, not padding.
- **The same-service residual grows ~9%/yr** — adult-facility
  services ~$5.5M → ~$6.6M over four years — plus a
  litigation-insurance line the vendor priced into Amendment 3
  (~$170K/yr).
- **The contract's own census trigger sits unused.** Compensation
  increases trigger when jail population (MADP) exceeds 350 — but
  the vendor's July 2025 staffing matrix, attached to Amendment 6,
  is sized for **300 ADP**. Whether actual Lane County jail
  population ran near 300 or 350 across FY23–FY27 is the column
  needed to test whether growth tracked bodies or price. We do not
  hold the county census series; this note is published with that
  column open.
- **Procurement posture: effective sole-source.** The county's 2021
  solicitation drew exactly one response — Wellpath's — and the
  Board Order memo records that the county knows of no other
  qualified provider in Oregon. Prior cycles: Corizon (2010, ended
  in lawsuits), California Forensic Medical Group (2015, four bids,
  merged into Wellpath 2019).

## What this does and does not show

This note documents the contract's authorized ceiling and its
growth structure. It does **not** establish overbilling — the
payment data we hold runs at the contract's installment rate — and
it does not assert wrongdoing: the escalation mechanisms,
new-scope additions, and the sole-source award are all documented
county decisions. It does not include jail-population data (the
open column above), county appropriation-side decisions, or
services outside this contract.
